About Astar Aqua

We fit the filter, then we look after it

Most people buy a filter once and forget it, which is exactly how a filter stops working. We do it the other way round. Every job starts with a look at your water and your plumbing, and ends with a unit that is on our service list, with cartridges arriving before they are due.

Our engineers are employed, not subcontracted. If something is wrong six months after fitting, the person who answers the phone can send the person who did the work.
